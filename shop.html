Sometimes the perfect piece is one that hasn't been made yet. I occasionally accept special commissions for handmade pottery, whether it's a variation of a piece in my collection or an idea of your own. Tell me what you have in mind.

How commissions work

  • Availability: Requests are considered individually. Existing orders and studio commitments may mean I cannot accept a commission right away, or at all.
  • Estimate and approval: I will review your request and, if I can accommodate it, provide a proposed design, price, and estimated timeline for your approval.
  • Full prepayment: Approved commissions must be paid in full before any production begins. An inquiry does not create an order or reserve studio time.
  • Firm commitment: Commissioned pieces are made specifically for you, not on approval. Once production begins, commissions are noncancelable and nonrefundable for a change of mind; the payment is forfeited if you decide you no longer want the piece. Exceptions apply where required by law or if Garden Shed Clay fails to meet its agreed obligations.
  • Handmade character: Natural variations in shape, dimensions, glaze, and color are expected. Completion dates are estimates, as drying and firing can affect the schedule.
