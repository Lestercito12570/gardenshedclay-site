About the maker

Made slowly. Made to be used.

Garden Shed Clay is the studio of Philadelphia potter Lester Morris. The work brings together functional ceramics, gardening, and an appreciation for practical objects that become part of daily life.

Each piece is made in small batches, with attention to form, surface, and the natural variation that comes from working by hand.

Inside Garden Shed Clay

Studio practice

The finished object is only one part of the story. Clay, glaze, firing, reclaim, and repetition all shape the final piece.

01 / Material

Clay with character

Forms and surfaces are chosen to retain the tactile qualities of the material rather than erase them.

02 / Process

Small-batch making

Work is produced in focused studio runs, allowing each group of pieces to develop its own identity.

03 / Purpose

Useful every day

Pottery is designed to live in the kitchen, garden, and home—not simply sit on a shelf.
