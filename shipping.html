From My Studio to Your Home

Shipping & Buying Handmade Pottery

Pottery is made to be held, used, and enjoyed. I take the same care packing and shipping your pottery that I take making it. Here are a few things worth knowing before your piece makes its way from my studio to you.

01 · Domestic Shipping

Getting your pottery home safely

Each order is packed carefully with the realities of shipping handmade ceramics in mind. Pottery needs room, cushioning, and a sturdy box, and larger or more delicate pieces may require additional packing.

Orders of $150 or more ship free within the United States. Shipping for smaller orders is calculated according to the pieces in your order.

02 · More Than One Piece

Combined shipping

Buying several pieces does not necessarily mean paying a full shipping charge for every piece. When pottery can travel safely together, I combine items into the same shipment whenever practical.

The shop is set up to reduce the additional shipping charge on multiple pieces. If an unusual order results in a meaningful shipping overage, I would rather return the difference than treat shipping as a source of profit.

03 · International Orders

Pottery travels

International shipping is available at a $65 flat shipping fee. International shipments may take longer to arrive and may be subject to customs duties, taxes, or import fees charged by the destination country.

Those destination-country charges, when applicable, are the responsibility of the buyer.

04 · Shipping Carrier

Need another carrier?

I generally use the shipping service that provides the most practical combination of safe handling, tracking, and cost for the order.

If you would prefer a particular carrier or shipping service, please contact me before ordering. I am happy to look at the available options, but additional shipping charges will apply when the requested service costs more.

05 · If Something Breaks

Please keep the box

I pack pottery carefully, but ceramics and shipping carriers occasionally have disagreements.

If a piece arrives damaged, please contact me promptly and keep all of the packaging. I will need clear photographs of the damaged pottery, the packing materials, and the outside of the shipping box, including any visible damage. These photographs are important when a shipping claim needs to be filed.

Please do not discard the box or broken pieces until we have resolved the issue.

06 · Handmade Means Handmade

No two pieces are quite alike

Clay is a natural material, and pottery goes through several transformations before it reaches you. Pieces are formed, dried, glazed, and fired at high temperatures. Small variations in shape, glaze color, surface, texture, and dimensions are normal parts of handmade ceramics.

Natural materials in the clay and glaze can also behave differently during firing. Tiny variations, speckles, marks, or changes in glaze are often evidence of the materials and firing process rather than flaws.

That individuality is part of why I make pottery by hand: your piece is your piece.

07 · Questions

Ask before you buy

If you would like another photograph, need a measurement, are buying a gift, have a shipping question, or simply want to know more about a piece, please ask.

You can reach me at lester@gardenshedclay.com . I would much rather answer a question before a piece leaves the studio than have you surprised when it arrives.

Made by hand. Packed by hand.

Every Garden Shed Clay piece has already made quite a journey through clay, glaze, and fire before it ever reaches a shipping box. My goal is to make the last part of that journey just as carefully.
